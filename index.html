Privacy Policy

Memes Culture (Android and iPhone apps) · Effective October 30, 2026

This policy explains how the Memes Culture app ("the app"), developed by Srivardhan Bhogadi, handles information. In short: the app does not ask you to create an account, does not show ads, does not use analytics or tracking, and does not collect personal information.

Information the app does not collect

The app does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, files, device identifiers or advertising ID. It does not ask for any permissions beyond internet access.

Information stored only on your device

To remember your preferences, the app saves the following on your device:

  • Sounds you mark as favorites
  • The volume you set for each sound
  • The order you arrange sounds in

This information never leaves your device except through your phone's own backup feature, if you have it turned on. In that case it is included in your Google account backup (Android) or iCloud backup (iPhone) under Google's or Apple's privacy policy. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Loading sounds over the internet

The list of sounds and the audio files are downloaded from a server hosted by Supabase. As with any internet request, the server receives technical information needed to deliver the content, such as your IP address and the time of the request, and may keep it briefly in standard server logs. The app does not send any information that identifies you, and this data is not used to track you or for advertising.

Requesting a sound by email

If you use "Request a Sound", the app opens your own email app with a pre-filled message. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it. If you do, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to read and reply to your request. We do not share it or add you to any mailing list. You can ask us to delete your emails at any time.

Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or share personal information with anyone.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Content and copyright

If you believe a sound in the app infringes your rights, email us with details and we will review and remove it where appropriate.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
