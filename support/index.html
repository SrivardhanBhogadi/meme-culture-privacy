Common questions

Sounds won't load. The app needs an internet connection to load sounds. Check your connection and tap Try Again.

I can't hear anything. Turn up your phone's volume, then open the ☰ menu: each sound has its own volume slider, which may be set low.

How do I rearrange buttons? On All Sounds with the search empty, press and hold a button, then drag it to a new spot. The order is saved automatically.

How do I save favorites? Tap the star on any button. Your favorites appear under the Favorites tab.
